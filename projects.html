Industry · Mine GIS
Mine Stability Analysis — Goedehoop Colliery

GIS-based underground stability assessment using subsidence, seam depth and geotechnical parameters to classify stable, moderately stable and high-risk areas and inform post-mining land use.

Featured
ContextGoedehoop North · 2024–2025
FocusRisk modelling + spatial analysis
OutcomeRisk classification + rehabilitation planning
Project Overview

The assessment integrated mine layout information and stability parameters through a GIS workflow. Spatial overlay and rule-based classification were used to identify risk zones. Approximately 353 hectares were identified as high risk, mainly associated with 2 seam workings, while more than 3,000 hectares were identified as suitable for controlled post-mining land use.

Wildlands

A geospatial restoration intelligence platform that combines satellite-derived vegetation monitoring, field observations, spatial context, analytics and reporting in one operational workflow.

Restoration Intelligence
DomainEcological restoration · Geospatial intelligence
MonitoringSatellite + field evidence
Decision SupportAnalytics + reporting
Project Overview

Wildlands is a geospatial monitoring and intelligence platform for ecological restoration. It connects restoration projects and monitoring areas with satellite-derived NDVI, field observations, spatial coordinates, maps, analytics and reporting. The platform enables users to monitor vegetation condition, identify areas requiring attention, validate satellite signals with field evidence and turn monitoring data into evidence-based reports for review and action.
