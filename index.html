02 // Projects
Featured Work
Geospatial Solutions
Industry · Mine GIS
Mine Stability Analysis — Goedehoop Colliery

Conducted a GIS-based underground stability assessment at Goedehoop Colliery (Dec 2024 – Jan 2025), using subsidence, seam depth, and geotechnical parameters to evaluate ground conditions. Spatial overlay and rule based classification were used to identify stable, moderately stable and high risk areas.
Approximately 353 hectares were identified as high risk, mainly linked to 2 seam workings, with additional smaller high risk areas from 5 seam and subsidence. More than 3000 hectares were found suitable for controlled post mining land use.
